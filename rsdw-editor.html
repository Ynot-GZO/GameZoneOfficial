Character summary
Unlocks

Unlocks recipes, buildings, and spells from a real progressed character save (safe for loading). One click.

Vitals

Health, stamina, and survival needs. Values are what the save currently stores.

Worn equipment

Click a slot to edit it. The game has 5 armour slots: Head, Chest, Legs, Cape, and one Trinket — rings and amulets share that trinket slot (only one at a time). Give set replaces Head, Chest, and Legs.

Add items

Click any filled or empty slot to edit it. Or use this form to fill a range of bag slots at once.
